UAERP Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

UAERP is a Discord bot that provides welcome messages, member departure logging, role synchronization between configured Discord servers, and role restoration for returning members.

2. Information We Process

Depending on enabled features, UAERP may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, role IDs, and membership events. It may store role information needed to restore eligible members' roles.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to send welcome messages, log member departures, synchronize roles between authorized servers, and restore saved roles when members rejoin.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord or shared when legally required or necessary to operate and protect the service.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as long as needed for the relevant features. Users can contact the bot owner to ask questions or request deletion of stored information.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the UAERP bot owner through the bot's official support server.
